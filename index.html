YTAS Autopilot

YTAS Autopilot is a private tool that its owner runs on their own computer to publish short educational videos to the owner's own YouTube channels: Pricetag (money and company history), ASDF Health (nutrition education) and ASDF Bible (Bible stories with public-domain text and art).

The tool signs in with Google only to the owner's channels. It uploads finished videos, schedules when they go public, and reads view counts and watch-time statistics of those videos. It is not offered to other users.
