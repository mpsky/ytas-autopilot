Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

YTAS Autopilot ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

What Google data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload videos the owner made and set their title, description and publish time.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel it is signed in to and read public statistics of the uploaded videos (views, likes, comments).
  • YouTube Analytics read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read watch-time statistics of the owner's own videos.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to decide which kinds of videos to make next. OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. The app has no server, does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token files removes all stored Google data.
